about=This plugin provides a modular and extensible framework for developing, comparing, and enhancing tour and route planning algorithms. It is designed to support multiple planning strategies, allowing algorithms to be implemented, swapped, and evaluated independently through well-defined interfaces. The plugin enables systematic experimentation with different optimization approaches, heuristics, and constraints, making it suitable for both research and real-world applications. Its flexible architecture supports easy integration into existing systems and encourages the extension of core functionality with custom algorithms, data sources, and evaluation methods. By separating algorithm logic from application logic, the plugin facilitates rapid prototyping, benchmarking, and continuous improvement of tour and route planning solutions across a wide range of use cases.
